# Currency and calendar

All amounts are reported in Swiss francs. The European web shop sells in euros; Silver converts with a fixed
planning rate of 0.94 franc per euro, the rate used by finance for the 2026 budget. Daily market rates are not applied.

Dates follow the Europe/Zurich time zone. The ISO week is used for weekly reports, and the month is the calendar month.
Store tills export timestamps in the Swiss format dd.mm.yyyy HH:mm:ss; the web shop and the ERP use ISO format.
